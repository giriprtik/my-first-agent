# Mount Everest Report

- Official height: 8,848.86 meters (29,031.7 feet) above sea level, jointly announced by Nepal and China in December 2020.
- Location: Situated in the Himalayas along the border between Nepal and Tibet (China).
- First successful summit: Reached on May 29, 1953.
