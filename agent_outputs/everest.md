# Mount Everest Facts

- Mount Everest stands at an official elevation of 29,032 feet (8,849 meters), making it the highest mountain in the world above sea level.
- The first confirmed successful ascent to the summit was achieved by Sir Edmund Hillary and Tenzing Norgay on May 29, 1953.
- The mountain continues to grow by approximately 4 millimeters each year due to the ongoing tectonic collision between the Indian and Eurasian plates.
